Before you book

Questions families often ask.

How much life insurance coverage do I need?

There is no single number that fits everyone. Consider mortgage and other debts, income replacement, dependents, childcare, education goals, existing savings, current coverage, and what your household can afford.

Is mortgage protection the same as private mortgage insurance?

No. Life insurance used for mortgage protection may provide a death benefit to a named beneficiary. Private mortgage insurance generally protects the lender against certain loan losses. Product structures and terms differ.

Do I have to buy a policy after speaking with you?

No. A conversation is an opportunity to ask questions and understand options. You can take time to review any proposed coverage before deciding.

Can I get life insurance if I have health conditions?

Possibly. Eligibility and pricing depend on the insurer’s underwriting guidelines and your individual circumstances. Only the insurer can determine an application outcome.
