Know the responsibilities and the realities.

Insurance sales requires learning, prospecting, follow-up, ethical conduct, and ongoing professional development. Compensation structures, expenses, appointments, licensing, and business results vary. Income is not guaranteed, and this is not a promise of employment or earnings.

Questions worth asking

Get the details before deciding.

Do I need a license?

Individuals who sell, solicit, or negotiate insurance generally need the appropriate state license. Exact requirements depend on the state and line of authority.

Is income guaranteed?

No. Compensation may depend on production, carrier and agency contracts, persistency, chargebacks, and other terms. Ask for written details about compensation, costs, and expectations.

Will I receive training?

Ask us about available onboarding, mentorship, product training, and compliance resources. Training does not replace licensing or required supervision.

Is this an employee job?

The relationship and work arrangement depend on the specific agreement. Ask for details about employee or independent-contractor status, expenses, taxes, and responsibilities before proceeding.
